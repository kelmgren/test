Technology export controls

MOFCOM consultation draft. Adoption and effective date have not been verified.

What the categories mean

If adopted:

Prohibition 禁止出口

Covered technology cannot be exported.

Restriction 限制出口

Export requires a technology export licence.

Approval is required and is not guaranteed. Completing the registration form does not itself authorize an export.

Coverage depends on each entry’s thresholds, purpose limitations and exclusions.

Scope and legal basis

These categories concern technology transfers, which can include know-how, licensing and technical services. The particular transaction must fall within the entry’s scope.

For example, the proposed therapeutic-platform entries 262704J and 262705J cover ownership transfers. Their notes exclude licensing without ownership transfer and specified outsourced R&D and production services from those entries.

说明：禁止出口类技术在管控范围内不得出口；限制出口类技术须取得技术出口许可，许可并非必然获批。填写登记表本身不构成出口许可。是否纳入管控须结合各条目的指标、用途限制及排除情形判断。本附件为征求意见稿，采纳情况及生效日期尚未核实。
